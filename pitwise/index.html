What PitWise does

Perfect on a second monitor next to the game. Everything live, read from the game's telemetry.

📻 Team radio like F1

Hold a key (mappable to a wheel button), talk, release — the engineer answers by voice. Works even while the game has focus. Speech recognised by OpenAI's Whisper, offline — no cloud.

🧠 Answers with no internet

Fuel, laps, tyres, pace, pit, position, records — he answers all of that instantly from telemetry, free and offline. Optionally connect AI for free-form chat — a free Google Gemini key, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI.

🎭 Your engineer, your rules

Give him a name, pick a personality (pro / tough guy / buddy / analyst), a language, a voice and speech rate. The tough guy tells you “Fuel's gone. Box, now!”

🗣️ Spotter & coach

“Careful, he's on your RIGHT!” — calls cars alongside you, advises wheel-to-wheel, warns about lock-ups and tyre abuse. Overtaken? He fires you up — the tough guy roasts you. Overtake? He praises you.

⏱️ Where you lose time

The track split into 10 mini-sectors. After each lap: “Sector 4: down 0.6s — brake later and carry more mid-corner speed.” Specific advice, not just numbers.

🏋️ Runs practice & qualifying

In practice he walks you through a plan, reads your pressures and gives a setup verdict. In qualifying he watches tyre warm-up (“tyres in the window — go NOW!”), fuel, and tells you whether to park it or try again.

🔧 Writes a setup into the game (ACC)

Say “prepare a setup” — he takes your setup, sets stint fuel, trims pressures from measured data and saves it into ACC. Just load it in-game.

⛽ Fuel strategy

From real data: how many laps you'll last, how much to refuel, how many stops. He calls it over the radio himself — “you're two laps short, save or box”.

🗺️ Track map

Draws itself during your first lap from your car's position (AC, ACC, LMU/rF2), saves it and loads instantly next time — with numbered corners, a colour speed heatmap and a live dot. In AC it even loads the exact track width and ideal line straight from the game's files.

🏆 Records & references

Personal best for every track and car, kept forever. On known circuits (Monza, Spa, Nordschleife…) you see an approximate GT3 reference lap — you know where to push.

📈 Pro-level telemetry

Speed, throttle, brake and steering traces for the last 30s, a G-meter, tyre temps and wear, pedal bars, race position.

FAQ

How does talking to the engineer work?

In the app you set a push-to-talk key (mappable to a wheel button, e.g. via MOZA Pit House). Hold — talk — release. Your speech is transcribed by Whisper (OpenAI's top recogniser) running offline on your PC. The engineer answers by voice within seconds.

Do I need an account or an AI key?

No. Racing questions (fuel, laps, tyres, pace, pit, position, records) are answered for free, straight from telemetry. An AI key is an optional bonus for free-form chat — a Google Gemini key is completely free (aistudio.google.com, no card), or use an Anthropic Claude / OpenAI key (cents per question, paid directly to them, nothing to us).

Will I get banned for using it?

No. PitWise doesn't touch the game — it only reads the data the game itself exposes via shared memory (the official telemetry). There's nothing for anti-cheat to flag.

Which games are supported?

Assetto Corsa & ACC — full support (including the track map and setup writing in ACC). Le Mans Ultimate & rFactor 2 — supported: map with numbered corners, sectors, live delta, lap history, rival names and strategy; the required telemetry plugin ships in the package and PitWise offers to install it for you in one click. F1 23 — enable Settings → Telemetry → UDP (port 20777) and PitWise detects it. iRacing — works out of the box; the spotter even uses the game's exact data.

Which languages does it support?

The engineer's voice and callouts work in 29 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Dutch, Czech, Slovak, Russian, Turkish and more). The app interface is available in 9 languages. You talk to him and he answers in your language — all offline.

Windows showed a blue box (SmartScreen) on first launch. What now?

That's normal for any new app from a small developer — Windows just doesn't know it yet. Click “More info” → “Run anyway” (it's a one-time thing). The package also includes a one-click fix script and a script-launched version that SmartScreen doesn't touch. PitWise contains nothing harmful — it installs nothing and only reads the game's telemetry.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 14 days and we'll refund you. No questions, no forms.

Do I have to install anything or run as admin?

No. Download one file, run it, done. No installation, no admin.

Where should I keep it while racing?

Best on a second monitor next to the game. There's also an “Always on top” toggle if you run a single monitor in windowed mode.
